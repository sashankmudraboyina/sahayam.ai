# Government Welfare Schemes Dataset
### Central Government · Telangana State · Andhra Pradesh State

**Purpose:** This document compiles welfare/benefit schemes run by the Government of India, the Government of Telangana, and the Government of Andhra Pradesh, for use as a structured dataset. For every scheme it lists: the launching authority, the target class/section of people, the benefit, the eligibility criteria, and the documents required.

**Compiled:** September 2026. Scheme amounts, portal URLs, and eligibility rules change frequently — before relying on this for an official application, verify the current rule on the scheme's official `.gov.in` portal (linked under each entry where known). Andhra Pradesh in particular renamed most of its previous "YSR/Jagananna/Amma Vodi" scheme brand after the June 2024 change of government (YSRCP → TDP-JSP-BJP/NDA coalition); Telangana renamed several "KCR/BRS-era" schemes after its December 2023 change of government (BRS → Congress). Where a scheme was renamed, both the old and new names are given.

**Legend used in every entry:**
- **Also known as** — Telugu/local name or previous name, where applicable
- **Category** — the class/section of people it targets
- **Launched by / Year**
- **Benefit** — what the beneficiary receives
- **Eligibility** — who qualifies
- **Documents required**
- **Official portal**

## Part 1 — Central Government Schemes

### 1.1 Agriculture & Farmer Welfare

#### 1. PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
- **Category:** Small and marginal landholding farmer families
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India / December 2018
- **Benefit:** ₹6,000/year paid in 3 equal instalments of ₹2,000 (Apr–Jul, Aug–Nov, Dec–Mar) directly to the Aadhaar-linked bank account of the farmer family (one benefit per family unit, not per member)
- **Eligibility:** Landholding farmer family cultivating agricultural land, of any size. Excluded: institutional landholders; former/current holders of constitutional posts; former/current ministers, MPs, MLAs/MLCs, mayors, district panchayat chairpersons; serving/retired government employees (with some exceptions for Class IV/Group D); income-tax payers; professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs) practising professionally
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, land ownership/record documents (Bhu Bharati/RoR), bank passbook (Aadhaar-seeded), mobile number, passport-size photo
- **Official portal:** pmkisan.gov.in

#### 2. PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- **Category:** All farmers (loanee and non-loanee), sharecroppers and tenant farmers
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / 18 February 2016
- **Benefit:** Low-premium crop insurance against yield loss from natural calamities, pests and diseases (farmer pays 2% for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi, 5% for commercial/horticultural crops; balance premium subsidised by Centre + State); claims aim to settle within ~2 months of harvest; recently expanded to cover crop damage by wild animals
- **Eligibility:** Any farmer (owner or tenant) growing a notified crop in a notified area; KYC-compliant Aadhaar-linked bank account mandatory
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, land ownership/tenancy record (7/12 extract or equivalent), bank passbook, sowing declaration/crop details
- **Official portal:** pmfby.gov.in

#### 3. Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- **Category:** Farmers, animal husbandry and fisheries workers
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Finance / 1998 (revised multiple times)
- **Benefit:** Short-term crop loan up to ₹3 lakh at an effective interest rate of ~4% per annum (after interest subvention + prompt repayment incentive); pre-approved by the lending bank for landholding farmers
- **Eligibility:** Farmer, tenant farmer, sharecropper, or member of a Self-Help Group/Joint Liability Group engaged in agriculture, animal husbandry or fisheries
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, land records, passport-size photo, bank account details
- **Official portal:** Through any scheduled commercial bank / RRB / cooperative bank

#### 4. PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY)
- **Category:** Small and marginal farmers (age 18–40)
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / 2019
- **Benefit:** Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹3,000 after age 60; government matches the subscriber's monthly contribution (₹55–₹200 depending on entry age)
- **Eligibility:** Small/marginal farmer owning up to 2 hectares, aged 18–40, not covered by NPS/ESIC/EPFO or another statutory social security scheme, not an income-tax payer
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, land record (khatauni), bank passbook, age proof
- **Official portal:** maandhan.in

### 1.2 Health & Medical Insurance

#### 5. Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY)
- **Category:** Economically vulnerable families (identified via SECC 2011); now also all senior citizens 70+
- **Launched by / Year:** National Health Authority, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare / 23 September 2018
- **Benefit:** ₹5 lakh/family/year cashless and paperless hospitalisation cover (secondary + tertiary care, ~1,929 procedures across 27 specialities) at empanelled public/private hospitals nationwide, with post-discharge medicine cover for 15 days; from September 2024, all citizens aged 70+ get free coverage regardless of income (Ayushman Vay Vandana Card)
- **Eligibility:** Families meeting SECC-2011 deprivation criteria (kutcha housing, no adult member 16–59, female-headed household with no adult male, disabled member with no able-bodied adult, manual scavenger family, primitive tribal group, legally released bonded labour) or workers in 11 specified occupational categories (construction, domestic work, street vending, etc.); separately, every citizen aged 70+ irrespective of income (cannot combine with CGHS/ECHS — must choose one)
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, ration card, SECC verification / family ID, mobile number
- **Official portal:** pmjay.gov.in / beneficiary.nha.gov.in

#### 6. Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)
- **Category:** Pregnant women (especially BPL, SC/ST)
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare / 2005
- **Benefit:** Cash assistance for institutional delivery — ₹1,400 (rural) / ₹1,000 (urban) for BPL/SC/ST women, plus ₹300–₹600 incentive to the ASHA worker for facilitating institutional delivery
- **Eligibility:** Pregnant woman delivering in a government-empanelled health facility; priority to BPL, SC/ST women and those in Low Performing States
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, BPL/ration card, ANC (antenatal care) card, bank passbook
- **Official portal:** nhm.gov.in

### 1.3 Housing

#### 7. PM Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G)
- **Category:** Rural houseless / kutcha-house families
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Rural Development / 2016 (PMAY-G 2.0 expansion approved August 2024)
- **Benefit:** ₹1.20 lakh (plain areas) / ₹1.30 lakh (hilly/difficult/IAP districts) for pucca house construction, paid in installments tied to construction progress, plus convergence support — ~90–95 days of MGNREGA wages for construction labour and assistance for a toilet under Swachh Bharat Mission
- **Eligibility:** Household is houseless or living in a 0/1/2-room kutcha house, identified through SECC-2011/Awaas+ survey data verified by the Gram Sabha; priority to SC/ST households, minorities, widows, persons with disabilities, freed bonded labourers, and families of martyred defence/paramilitary personnel; beneficiary selection is via survey, not open application
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, MGNREGA job card, bank account (Aadhaar-linked), Swachh Bharat Mission number, caste certificate (if applicable), no-pucca-house self-declaration
- **Official portal:** pmayg.nic.in

#### 8. PM Awas Yojana – Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0)
- **Category:** Urban poor and middle-income families without a pucca house
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs / approved August 2024, effective 1 September 2024, mission period through 2029
- **Benefit:** Central assistance through four verticals — Beneficiary-Led Construction, Affordable Housing in Partnership, Affordable Rental Housing, and Interest Subsidy Scheme (4% interest subsidy on home loans up to ₹25 lakh for EWS/LIG/MIG, capped near ₹1.80 lakh benefit)
- **Eligibility:** EWS/LIG/MIG urban family (MIG annual income capped near ₹9 lakh under 2.0, reduced from ₹18 lakh under PMAY-U 1.0) with no pucca house owned anywhere in India by any family member, and no prior central housing assistance availed; special focus on widows, single women, senior citizens, persons with disabilities, SC/ST, minorities and transgender persons
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, bank passbook, income certificate, residence proof, ration card, no-pucca-house affidavit, caste certificate (if applicable)
- **Official portal:** pmay-urban.gov.in

### 1.4 Women & Child Development

#### 9. PM Ujjwala Yojana 2.0 (PMUY)
- **Category:** Women from poor/BPL households, including migrant families
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas / May 2016 (2.0 expansion 2021)
- **Benefit:** Deposit-free LPG gas connection (worth ~₹1,600) with a free first refill and free stove/hotplate; choice of 5 kg or 14.2 kg cylinder
- **Eligibility:** Adult woman (18+), Indian citizen, from a BPL/SECC-2011-poor household with no existing LPG connection in the name of any family member; under 2.0, migrant families without permanent address proof can self-declare in place of standard documents
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, BPL/SECC ration card or poverty declaration, bank passbook, passport-size photo; migrants may substitute a self-declaration
- **Official portal:** pmuy.gov.in

#### 10. PM Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)
- **Category:** Pregnant and lactating mothers
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Women & Child Development / 2017 (under the National Food Security Act, 2013)
- **Benefit:** ₹5,000 cash for the first living child (in 2 instalments, tied to ANC registration and delivery), and ₹6,000 for the second child if it is a girl — paid via DBT
- **Eligibility:** Pregnant/lactating woman (18+) for her first living child, and second child only if a girl; excludes government/PSU employees receiving paid maternity leave
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card (mother), MCP (Mother and Child Protection) card, bank passbook, marriage certificate (in some states)
- **Official portal:** pmmvy.wcd.gov.in

#### 11. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
- **Category:** Girl children (below age 10)
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Finance / 2015 (part of Beti Bachao Beti Padhao)
- **Benefit:** Small-savings account with 8.2% p.a. interest (revised quarterly), full income-tax exemption under Section 80C, maturity at 21 years from account opening; minimum ₹250/year, maximum ₹1.5 lakh/year deposit
- **Eligibility:** Girl child below 10 years of age at account opening; maximum two accounts per family (three in case of twins/triplets in the second birth)
- **Documents required:** Girl child's birth certificate, guardian's Aadhaar and PAN, address proof
- **Official portal:** Via post offices and authorised banks; india.gov.in

#### 12. Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)
- **Category:** Girl children nationwide
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Women & Child Development / 22 January 2015
- **Benefit:** Not a direct cash-transfer scheme — funds awareness campaigns, gender-sensitisation, and strengthens enforcement against sex-selective practices; feeds into schemes like Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana
- **Eligibility:** N/A (a national campaign, not an individual-application scheme)
- **Documents required:** N/A
- **Official portal:** wcd.nic.in

### 1.5 Financial Inclusion, Insurance & Pension

#### 13. PM Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)
- **Category:** Unbanked citizens
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Finance / 28 August 2014
- **Benefit:** Zero-balance savings bank account with a RuPay debit card carrying ₹2 lakh accidental insurance cover, overdraft facility up to ₹10,000, and gateway access to DBT, PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY and Mudra loans
- **Eligibility:** Any Indian citizen without a bank account, above 10 years of age
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card (or any OVD — officially valid document); self-certification accepted if current address differs from Aadhaar address
- **Official portal:** pmjdy.gov.in

#### 14. PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)
- **Category:** Bank/post office account holders, age 18–70
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Finance / 9 May 2015
- **Benefit:** ₹2 lakh accidental death/full disability cover, ₹1 lakh for partial disability, for an annual premium of ₹20 (auto-debited)
- **Eligibility:** Savings bank/post office account holder aged 18–70 with auto-debit consent
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar-linked bank account, consent form
- **Official portal:** jansuraksha.gov.in

#### 15. PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)
- **Category:** Bank/post office account holders, age 18–50
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Finance / 9 May 2015
- **Benefit:** ₹2 lakh life insurance cover (any cause of death) for an annual premium of ~₹436, auto-renewable up to age 55
- **Eligibility:** Bank/post office account holder aged 18–50 (can join up to 50; cover continues to 55 with auto-renewal)
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar-linked bank account, consent/auto-debit form
- **Official portal:** jansuraksha.gov.in

#### 16. Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
- **Category:** Unorganised sector workers, age 18–40
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Finance / 9 May 2015
- **Benefit:** Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000/2,000/3,000/4,000/5,000 after age 60 (based on contribution amount and entry age); spouse receives same pension after subscriber's death; corpus returned to nominee thereafter
- **Eligibility:** Indian citizen aged 18–40 with a savings bank/post office account
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, bank passbook, mobile number
- **Official portal:** npscra.nsdl.co.in / eNPS portal

#### 17. PM Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM)
- **Category:** Unorganised sector workers (age 18–40, monthly income ≤ ₹15,000)
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Labour & Employment / 2019
- **Benefit:** Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹3,000 after age 60; government contributes an equal matching share (₹55–₹200/month based on entry age)
- **Eligibility:** Unorganised worker (street vendor, domestic worker, rickshaw puller, construction worker, etc.), age 18–40, monthly income up to ₹15,000, not covered under NPS/ESIC/EPFO, not an income-tax payer
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, savings bank account/Jan Dhan account, mobile number
- **Official portal:** maandhan.in

#### 18. National Pension System (NPS)
- **Category:** All citizens, including unorganised-sector and self-employed persons
- **Launched by / Year:** PFRDA, Ministry of Finance / 2004 (opened to all citizens 2009)
- **Benefit:** Market-linked, tax-efficient retirement corpus; at age 60, 60% can be withdrawn as tax-free lump sum and 40% must be used to purchase an annuity for lifelong pension
- **Eligibility:** Any Indian citizen aged 18–70
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, PAN card, bank account details
- **Official portal:** enps.nsdl.com / cra-nsdl.com

#### 19. Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC)
- **Category:** Women and girls
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Finance / 2023
- **Benefit:** Small-savings certificate offering ~7.5% p.a. interest on deposits up to ₹2 lakh, 2-year tenure, partial withdrawal facility
- **Eligibility:** Any woman or girl (account can be opened by a guardian for a minor girl)
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, address proof
- **Official portal:** Via post offices and authorised banks

### 1.6 Employment, Skilling & MSME / Self-Employment

#### 20. PM Vishwakarma Yojana
- **Category:** Traditional artisans and craftspeople (18 notified trades)
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of MSME / 17 September 2023
- **Benefit:** PM Vishwakarma recognition certificate + ID card; ₹15,000 toolkit incentive; free basic + advanced skill training with ₹500/day stipend; collateral-free loans up to ₹3 lakh at 5% concessional interest (₹1 lakh in first tranche, ₹2 lakh in second); digital transaction incentive of ₹1/transaction (up to 100/month); marketing and branding support
- **Eligibility:** Age 18+, working with hands/tools in one of the 18 family-based trades (carpenter, blacksmith, goldsmith, potter, cobbler, tailor, mason, barber, weaver, boat maker, etc.), self-employed in the unorganised sector, not availed a similar government self-employment loan (PMEGP/PM SVANidhi/Mudra) in the last 5 years; one member per family; government employees and their families excluded
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, trade/profession proof, bank account details, passport-size photo
- **Official portal:** pmvishwakarma.gov.in

#### 21. PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana
- **Category:** Residential electricity consumers/households
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of New & Renewable Energy / February 2024
- **Benefit:** Up to 300 units/month of free electricity via rooftop solar; subsidy of ₹30,000–₹78,000 depending on system size (1–3 kW)
- **Eligibility:** Residential household with a suitable rooftop and an electricity connection
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, electricity bill/consumer number, bank account (Aadhaar-linked), roof ownership proof
- **Official portal:** pmsuryaghar.gov.in

#### 22. PM SVANidhi
- **Category:** Urban street vendors
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs / 1 June 2020
- **Benefit:** Escalating collateral-free working-capital loans — ₹10,000 (1st tranche) → ₹20,000 (2nd) → ₹50,000 (3rd), with 7% annual interest subvention and cashback incentives for digital transactions
- **Eligibility:** Street vendor with a Certificate of Vending/Identity Card issued by Urban Local Body, or included in the ULB survey list
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, Certificate of Vending / vendor survey ID, bank account details
- **Official portal:** pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in

#### 23. e-Shram Card
- **Category:** Unorganised sector workers
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Labour & Employment / 26 August 2021
- **Benefit:** Free 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN); automatic ₹2 lakh accidental insurance cover under PMSBY; convergence gateway to PM-SYM pension, PM-JAY and other welfare schemes
- **Eligibility:** Unorganised sector worker aged 16–59 (domestic worker, construction worker, street vendor, agricultural labourer, gig/platform worker, etc.), not an EPFO/ESIC member or income-tax payer
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, bank account details, mobile number
- **Official portal:** eshram.gov.in

#### 24. MGNREGA / NREGA (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act)
- **Category:** Rural households (adult members willing to do unskilled manual work)
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Rural Development / 2005
- **Benefit:** Legal guarantee of 100 days of wage employment per rural household per financial year; wage rates vary by state (approx. ₹247–₹267+/day, revised annually)
- **Eligibility:** Adult member of a rural household willing to do unskilled manual work, registered with the local Gram Panchayat
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, MGNREGA job card application, residence proof, bank/post office account
- **Official portal:** nrega.nic.in

#### 25. PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana 4.0 (PMKVY)
- **Category:** Youth seeking skill certification/employment
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship / 2015 (4.0 launched 2023)
- **Benefit:** Free short-term skill training aligned to National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF), NSQF certification, and a monetary reward up to ₹8,000 on successful assessment
- **Eligibility:** Indian citizen, typically 15–45 years, school/college dropout or unemployed youth
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, education certificates, bank account details
- **Official portal:** pmkvyofficial.org

#### 26. PM DAKSH Yojana
- **Category:** SC, ST, OBC, EBC, DNT and safai karamchari persons
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment / 2021
- **Benefit:** Free skill development/up-skilling training with a stipend of ₹1,000–₹3,000/month during the training period
- **Eligibility:** SC/ST/OBC/EBC/DNT/safai karamchari individual, typically aged 18–45, meeting the income criteria for the target category
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, caste certificate, income certificate, bank account details
- **Official portal:** pmdaksh.dosje.gov.in

#### 27. DAY-NRLM (Ajeevika – National Rural Livelihood Mission)
- **Category:** Rural women in Self-Help Groups (SHGs)
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Rural Development / 2011
- **Benefit:** Revolving Fund of ₹15,000 per SHG, Community Investment Fund up to ₹2.5 lakh, and bank-linkage loans up to ₹3 lakh at subsidised interest for SHG members
- **Eligibility:** Rural woman who is a member of (or forms) a registered Self-Help Group under NRLM
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, SHG membership/registration proof, bank account details
- **Official portal:** aajeevika.gov.in

#### 28. Stand-Up India
- **Category:** SC/ST and women entrepreneurs
- **Launched by / Year:** Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance / 5 April 2016
- **Benefit:** Bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore for setting up a greenfield enterprise (manufacturing, services, trading, or agri-allied)
- **Eligibility:** SC/ST and/or woman entrepreneur above 18 years, setting up a new (greenfield) enterprise; at least 51% shareholding/controlling stake must rest with the SC/ST/woman promoter for non-individual enterprises
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar/PAN card, caste certificate (if applicable), project report, business plan
- **Official portal:** standupmitra.in

#### 29. Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)
- **Category:** Prospective entrepreneurs (individuals, 18+)
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of MSME / 2008
- **Benefit:** Credit-linked subsidy of 15–35% of the project cost (higher for rural/special categories) for setting up new micro-enterprises, up to a project cost of ₹50 lakh (manufacturing) / ₹20 lakh (service)
- **Eligibility:** Individual above 18 years, minimum Class 8 pass for projects above ₹10 lakh (manufacturing)/₹5 lakh (service); only new units are eligible
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, education certificate, project report, caste certificate (for subsidy category), bank account details
- **Official portal:** kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal

### 1.7 Education & Scholarships

#### 30. National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
- **Category:** SC/ST/OBC/Minority/EWS/PwD students
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Electronics & IT with concerned line ministries / 2015
- **Benefit:** Single-window access to 80+ central and state scholarships — Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, Merit-cum-Means, Top Class Education, and more — ranging roughly from ₹1,000 to ₹20,000+/year depending on the scheme and category
- **Eligibility:** Varies by individual scholarship scheme; broadly SC/ST/OBC/Minority/EWS/PwD students enrolled in a recognised school/college, subject to family income ceilings
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, caste certificate, income certificate, previous marksheet, bank account (Aadhaar-linked), bonafide/institution certificate
- **Official portal:** scholarships.gov.in

#### 31. PM YASASVI Scholarship
- **Category:** OBC, EBC and DNT (De-notified Tribe) students
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment / 2021
- **Benefit:** ₹75,000–₹1,25,000/year scholarship for meritorious OBC/EBC/DNT students (Classes 9–12 and beyond)
- **Eligibility:** OBC/EBC/DNT student clearing the YASASVI entrance test, family income within the prescribed ceiling
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, caste certificate, income certificate, previous academic records
- **Official portal:** yet.nta.ac.in / scholarships.gov.in

#### 32. PM POSHAN (erstwhile Mid-Day Meal Scheme)
- **Category:** Government/government-aided school students (Classes 1–8)
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Education / 1995 (renamed PM POSHAN in 2021)
- **Benefit:** Free hot cooked nutritious meal on all school working days to improve nutrition and school attendance
- **Eligibility:** Student enrolled in a government or government-aided school, Classes 1–8
- **Documents required:** School enrolment record (no separate application needed by the student)
- **Official portal:** pmposhan.education.gov.in

### 1.8 Rural Development, Infrastructure & Other Welfare

#### 33. National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)
- **Category:** BPL elderly, widows and persons with disability
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Rural Development / 1995
- **Benefit:** Old-age pension, widow pension and disability pension for BPL households (monthly amount varies by state, with central contribution as a base — typically ₹200–₹500 central share, topped up by states)
- **Eligibility:** BPL household; old-age pension for 60+ (79+ gets a higher central rate); widow pension for widows aged 40–79; disability pension for persons with 80%+ disability, aged 18–79
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, BPL certificate/ration card, age/widowhood/disability proof, bank passbook
- **Official portal:** nsap.nic.in

#### 34. Jal Jeevan Mission
- **Category:** Rural households
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Jal Shakti / 15 August 2019
- **Benefit:** Free functional household tap connection with a minimum of 55 litres per person per day of safe drinking water
- **Eligibility:** Rural household without a piped water connection
- **Documents required:** No individual application typically required — implemented village-wide; household provides address/residence proof for connection records
- **Official portal:** jaljeevanmission.gov.in

#### 35. PM Swamitva Yojana
- **Category:** Rural property owners (inhabited village land)
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Panchayati Raj / 24 April 2020
- **Benefit:** Legal Property Card (Adhikar Patra) based on drone survey of rural residential land, enabling the property to be used as collateral for a bank loan up to ₹5 lakh
- **Eligibility:** Owner/occupant of residential property in a surveyed rural inhabited (abadi) area
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, existing property/revenue documents (if any), residence proof
- **Official portal:** svamitva.nic.in

#### 36. PM Internship Scheme
- **Category:** Youth (21–24 years) seeking corporate work experience
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Corporate Affairs / 2024
- **Benefit:** 12-month internship in a top company with ₹5,000/month from the company + ₹4,000/month from the government (₹9,000/month total stipend) + one-time ₹6,000 travel/incidental grant
- **Eligibility:** Indian citizen aged 21–24, not employed full-time, not enrolled in a full-time course, from a family with no member earning above the prescribed income ceiling and no member being a permanent government employee
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, education certificates, bank account details
- **Official portal:** pminternship.mca.gov.in

#### 37. Agniveer Scheme (Agnipath)
- **Category:** Youth aged 17.5–21 seeking defence service
- **Launched by / Year:** Ministry of Defence / 14 June 2022

- **Category:** ITI / Polytechnic / Degree students
- **Launched by / Year:** Government of Andhra Pradesh / 2019

- **Eligibility:** AP student enrolled in ITI, polytechnic, or degree/professional courses, within the prescribed family income ceiling

#### 14. Jagananna Vasathi Deevena (వసతి దీవెన)

#### 15. AP Nadu-Nedu School Infrastructure Programme

### 3.5 Housing

#### 21. AP Navaratnalu Housing (PMAY-AP)

### 3.8 Senior Citizens, Disability & Social Security

#### 30. NTR Bharosa Pension Scheme (ఎన్టీఆర్ భరోసా పింఛను) — formerly YSR Pension Kanuka
- **Category:** Elderly, widows, disabled, transgender persons, Beedi workers, weavers, dappu artists, toddy tappers, cobblers, fisherfolk, HIV/AIDS-affected persons
- **Launched by / Year:** Government of Andhra Pradesh / July 2024 (renamed and enhanced from YSR Pension Kanuka, 2019)
- **Benefit:** Base pension raised from ₹3,000 to ₹4,000/month (July 2024) for elderly/widows; ₹6,000/month for partial disability; ₹15,000/month for total disability; ₹10,000/month for chronic-illness categories (e.g., chronic kidney disease, thalassemia); ~65 lakh beneficiaries statewide; DBT to Aadhaar-linked bank account
- **Eligibility:** AP resident aged 60+ (old age), widow of any age, person with 40%+ disability, or member of a specified occupational category (Beedi worker, weaver, toddy tapper, fisherman, etc.); annual family income below ₹1 lakh; not a government employee/pensioner
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, age proof, BPL ration card/income certificate, bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked), widow certificate (if applicable), disability certificate (if applicable), residence proof
- **Official portal:** navasakam2.ap.gov.in / sspensions.ap.gov.in

#### 31. AP Disability Pension Scheme
- **Category:** Persons with disability
- **Launched by / Year:** Government of Andhra Pradesh (part of the NTR Bharosa umbrella, listed separately for its base disability rate)
- **Benefit:** ₹3,000/month base disability pension for 40%+ disability (higher slabs up to ₹15,000/month for total disability under NTR Bharosa), DBT to bank account
- **Eligibility:** Person with 40%+ disability certified by a Medical Board, AP resident, within income limits
- **Documents required:** Aadhaar card, disability certificate, residence proof, bank passbook
- **Official portal:** navasakam2.ap.gov.in

## Notes, Caveats & Sources

1. **Volatility of scheme data:** Government scheme names, benefit amounts and eligibility thresholds change with almost every state budget and, in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, have changed substantially following the December 2023 and June 2024 changes of government respectively. Figures above reflect the most recent information available as of September 2026 research; always cross-check against the official `.gov.in` portal before using for an actual application.
2. **Scheme renames tracked in this document:**
   - Telangana: Rythu Bandhu → Rythu Bharosa (2025); Double Bedroom Scheme → Indiramma Indlu (2024–25)
   - Andhra Pradesh: YSR Pension Kanuka → NTR Bharosa Pension Scheme (2024); YSR Amma Vodi → Talliki Vandanam (2025); YSR Rythu Bharosa → Annadata Sukhibhava (2025–26)
3. **Common documents across almost all schemes:** Aadhaar card, residence/domicile proof, income certificate, caste/community certificate (where category-specific), bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked), passport-size photograph, ration card. These are listed per-scheme above but recur throughout.
4. **Coverage:** This dataset covers 38 Central schemes, 28 Telangana schemes, and 31 Andhra Pradesh schemes (97 total), spanning agriculture, health, housing, women & child welfare, financial inclusion & pension, employment & skilling, education & scholarships, and SC/ST/BC/minority/disability welfare. It is not exhaustive — hundreds of smaller central and state schemes exist beyond this list (e.g., sector-specific schemes for handicrafts, tribal welfare (ST-specific corporations), transgender welfare, and district-level pilot programmes) that can be added on request.
5. **Primary sources used:** Official scheme portals (pmkisan.gov.in, pmjay.gov.in, pmay-urban.gov.in, pmuy.gov.in, pmvishwakarma.gov.in, mahalakshmi.telangana.gov.in, aasara.telangana.gov.in, scwelfare.telangana.gov.in, telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in, rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in, navasakam2.ap.gov.in, apgramasachivalayam.ap.gov.in, jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in), Government of India press releases (PIB/newsonair.gov.in), and current-affairs/scheme-aggregator sources cross-verified against news coverage of the 2024–25 AP and Telangana budget announcements (The News Minute, The South First, The Wire, Deccan Herald, PTI/Careers360).
